# AI assistance and human contribution disclosure

This repository includes substantial AI-assisted design, C++/Python implementation,
tests, debugging, documentation and notebook drafting from interactions with ChatGPT.
AI assistance is not limited to spelling or prose editing. The pre-existing platform
also contains AI-assisted code. Do not represent the generated work as independently
written or independently validated by the student without performing that work.

For this foundation, assistance includes proposed research framing, direct depth
state-machine/storage/API code, a Python replay and capture helper, synthetic
fixtures, tests, and the Phase I notebook. Automated validation performed in the
development environment is distinct from the student's own understanding and actual
WSL/IBKR acceptance. No real broker data were accessed by this development process.

AMS 518 syllabus p.3 permits project AI use with clear acknowledgement and prohibits
AI for homework. This is project work, not homework. The supplied AMS 520 syllabus
does not specify an AI-use policy; obtain instructor guidance. Shared cross-course
use and group rules also require confirmation. No approval or teammate is invented.

Before submission, complete a dated human-contribution log: contributor, exact work,
reviewed commit, derivations understood/reproduced, tests run independently, observed
failures and fixes, and instructor conditions. The student remains responsible for
correctness, citations, claims, data rights, and the ability to explain the code.
