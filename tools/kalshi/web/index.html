The raw ensemble estimates YES settlement probability. With sensitivity haircut h, the displayed range is L = max(0, p − h) to U = min(1, p + h). Entry sizing uses pYES = L and pNO = 1 − U. These are separate directional stress estimates and need not sum to one.

What each trade bar measures
P&Lclose = closing proceeds − exit fees − removed entry basisremoved basis = entry cost + allocated entry fees

Results use confirmed fills and settlements. Account replay consumes entry lots in first-in, first-out (FIFO) order. Same-direction fills with identical timestamps are labeled as a simultaneous-fill batch. Its combined quantity, fees and closing result are exact; no ordering among those fills is invented. Partial consumption of a batch with differing entry costs remains unavailable when the allocation is ambiguous. Settlement quantity retains the venue-reported counts, while closing quantity and payout refer to independently replayed net exposure. Model paper results use the recorded ledger’s closing basis and realized P&L, including its average-cost allocations. Entry fees enter the holding’s basis and are allocated when quantity closes; they are not deducted a second time from the same realized result.

A purchase that only opens exposure has zero recognized realized P&L. A partial close recognizes the closed quantity, and a reversal can close one side while opening the other. Missing opening basis makes a closing result unavailable rather than zero. Multiple closing executions can belong to the same position; execution-level win statistics do not treat them as independent strategy trials.
