10 / Self-financing replication

Delta hedging & replication

SYNTHETIC · NO ORDERS

One short European payoff unit funded by its model premium. Compare no stock hedge, initial delta only, and periodic hedges on the same exact-GBM paths. Buying stock consumes cash; negative cash accrues borrowing cost. No capital is added to rescue a losing hedge.

Option and funding model

Separate path dynamics and costs

Rates/volatilities are decimals. Stock costs apply at entry, each nonzero rebalance, and liquidation. Fractional shares; no dividends, margin, stock-borrow fees, impact or asymmetric funding.
